Product rules
No payments, deposits or invoices. The app only shows service prices when the professional chooses to.
Records lock 24 hours after creation. Later changes are logged corrections with author and time.
Client habits (cancellations, no-shows) are private to the professional. The rate appears after 4 visits.
Lock screen alerts never show client names.
No-show protection is the professional's own rule: a limit from 1 to 5, counted only for her own clients. At the limit she chooses to be told, to approve each booking, or to pause online booking, and she can always book the client herself.
Every AI draft needs the professional to review before it is saved.
Clients can request a copy of their data or its deletion from their portal.
Cancellation and change rules belong to the professional: free-change window, what clients can do inside it, reschedule limit and reminder. Every client screen (booking page, before confirming, confirmation, manage page, cancel, reschedule) reads the same rules.
Clients have no app: the personal link in the WhatsApp message is their way back to cancel or reschedule. If they lose it, they find it again with their phone number and a code.
Existing appointments can always be managed, even when online booking is paused or needs approval.
Cancellations made by the professional never count as a missed appointment for the client.
Delay and blocked-day messages open in WhatsApp ready to send. The app never sends on the professional's behalf.
A paused booking page, or a client whose online booking is stopped, always offers a way to message the professional.
Guests are booked back to back with the same service, and the time adds up.
A service can list conditions it is not suitable for. The app warns the professional and never blocks a booking.
Quiet hours silence every alert except a client running late and same-day cancellations.
A touch-up or reaction check is linked to the earlier visit and does not ask for a new health form.
Health answers from a client are sealed in her browser with the professional's key. Servers only carry sealed envelopes and delete them once the professional's iPhone has them.
Records, notes, photos and recordings live on the professional's iPhone and in her iCloud. Servers hold bookings only: name, phone, day and time.
The recovery key is the only way back into sealed records if the iPhone and the iCloud Keychain are lost. Bello Hub never sees it.
The AI assistant runs on the iPhone. Without it, the professional fills the record by hand or dictates it. Cloud help is off by default and asks the client first.
The app, the booking page and the messages speak pt-BR, pt-PT, es-MX, es-ES or en-US. Currency belongs to the business; number style follows the reader's language.
