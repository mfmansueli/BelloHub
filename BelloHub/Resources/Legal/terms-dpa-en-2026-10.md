## 3. Price and free allowance
Your first 10 client registrations are free. Deleted clients still count toward this limit. Registrations are counted across manual entry, import and online booking; duplicates and edits do not count. After the tenth, adding another client needs a subscription. Existing clients, appointments and records always stay available and editable; we never delete or lock your data because you do not subscribe. The paid plan has unlimited client registrations and the same features monthly or yearly. Prices are shown in the App Store before you buy. Billing, renewal and cancellation are handled by Apple and follow Apple's rules; manage them in your Apple account settings.
