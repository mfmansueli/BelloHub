# Consent for photos of your treatment

**Who is asking.** {{PROFESSIONAL_NAME}} ("the professional") would like to take photos during your visits. Bello Hub is the app she uses to keep them.

**What this is.** With your permission she may photograph the treated area (for example before and after) to follow your results and plan your next visit.

**Private by default.** Photos stay in her private records on her iPhone and in her own iCloud, encrypted. They are not saved to her phone's camera roll and Bello Hub's servers never receive them. If you send her a photo yourself, for example in a skin check-in, it is also encrypted before it leaves your device.

**Choose what she may do. You can choose each one separately.**
☐ **Private records only.** Only the professional can see my photos.
☐ **Share with me.** She may show my photos to me on my client page.
☐ **Portfolio.** She may use my photos to show her work, for example on social media or her website. She will ask me before using any photo that could identify me.

**Changing your mind.** You can change or withdraw any choice at any time by telling the professional. Withdrawing does not affect the lawfulness of what was done before. She will stop using your photos for her portfolio and will delete them on request.

**Legal note.** Photos of you are personal data, and a photo of your skin or body can be special category data. This is your explicit consent, for the purposes you ticked, under the UK GDPR or GDPR (Article 9(2)(a)) or the equivalent law where you live.
