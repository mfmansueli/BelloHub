## Description (2218 / 4000)

```text
You're not alone anymore. Bello Hub, with you every step of the way.

Bello Hub is the diary and client record for independent beauty and aesthetic-health professionals: beauty therapists, hairdressers, nail technicians, lash and brow artists, barbers, tattoo and PMU artists, and massage therapists.

YOUR DIARY
• Day and week views of your appointments, time blocks and free slots
• Your hours, breaks, days off and buffer time, set once
• Ready-made WhatsApp messages for reminders and follow-ups: you tap send

A BOOKING LINK FOR YOUR CLIENTS
• Clients book from your link in any browser, on any phone or computer, with no app and no password
• You decide: instant booking or your approval, cancellation rules, notice and booking window
• The waiting list offers a freed slot to the people waiting, and no-show protection handles repeat absences
• Share the link or its QR code wherever your clients find you

A CLIENT RECORD THAT REMEMBERS
• Visit history, notes and habits for each client
• Before and after photos taken inside the app, never mixed with your camera roll
• Health forms your clients fill in before the visit, and the status of each consent
• Records lock 24 hours after the visit; later changes are kept as corrections
• Follow-ups by treatment interval, and a list of clients who haven't returned
• Export a client's record as a PDF, and all your data whenever you like

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your records stay on your iPhone and in your iCloud, encrypted by the app
• Health forms are sealed in your client's browser: Bello Hub can't read them
• Face ID lock and a 12-word recovery key
• No ads, no tracking, no analytics

NO MONEY IN THE MIDDLE
Bello Hub takes no payments and keeps no invoices. Prices are optional information on your services.

BELLO HUB PRO
Your first 10 clients are free. To register more, choose Bello Hub Pro, monthly or yearly. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless you turn it off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it in your App Store account settings.

Terms of Use: https://bellohub.app/legal/terms
Privacy Policy: https://bellohub.app/legal/privacy
```
