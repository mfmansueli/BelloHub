# Consent to the use of your health information

**Who is asking.** {{PROFESSIONAL_NAME}} ("the professional") is responsible for the information you share here. Bello Hub is the app she uses to keep her records.

**What you are agreeing to.** You will answer questions about your health (for example allergies, skin conditions, medication, pregnancy and recent procedures) and may add a signature. The professional uses your answers only to decide whether and how she can treat you safely, and to keep a record of your treatments.

**Who can read it.** Only the professional. Your answers are encrypted in your browser before they are sent, so Bello Hub cannot read them. Our servers keep the encrypted copy only until her iPhone has received it, and never more than 30 days. She keeps your answers in her private records on her iPhone and in her own iCloud.

**How long it is kept.** For as long as the professional keeps your client record. She may have professional or legal duties to keep records for a period. You can ask her for a copy, a correction or deletion at any time.

**Your choice.** Sharing this is optional. If you do not agree, tell the professional; she may not be able to offer some treatments safely. You can withdraw your consent at any time by contacting her. Withdrawing does not affect the lawfulness of what was done before.

**Legal basis.** Health information is special category data. In the United Kingdom and Ireland this is your explicit consent under Article 9(2)(a) of the UK GDPR or the GDPR. In Australia and New Zealand it is your consent to the collection and use of your sensitive health information under the Privacy Act. In South Africa it is your consent to the processing of special personal information under POPIA. You can ask the professional to access, correct or delete your information, and you can complain to the regulator in your country (the ICO in the United Kingdom, the Data Protection Commission in Ireland, the OAIC in Australia, the Privacy Commissioner in New Zealand, the Information Regulator in South Africa).
