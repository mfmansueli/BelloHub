# Your information: ask for a copy or deletion

**What this page is.** {{PROFESSIONAL_NAME}} keeps your name, contact details, appointments and, if you shared them, your health answers and photos. You can ask her for a copy, a correction or deletion at any time. This is also how you withdraw a consent.

**How to ask.** Choose what you want, write your name and phone number as you gave them when booking, and send. The request goes to her. She answers you directly, within one month at the latest (30 days).

**What can you ask for.**
- **Access:** a copy of what she holds about you, in a readable file.
- **Rectification** of something that is wrong.
- **Erasure** of your information. She may have to keep some records for a period under professional or tax rules; she will tell you what and for how long.
- **Withdrawal or objection** to a use you agreed to, such as photos in her portfolio.
- **Portability**, where the law gives it to you.

**What Bello Hub holds.** Only your booking details (name, phone, email, service, time, status), which are deleted from our servers 7 days after your visit. Your health answers and photos are not on our servers in readable form: they are with the professional. If you also want your booking details removed sooner, say so in the request.

**Identity.** To protect you, she may confirm that the request really comes from you, for example by sending a code to the email you booked with.

**If you are not satisfied.** You can complain to the regulator where you live: the ICO (United Kingdom), the Data Protection Commission (Ireland), the OAIC (Australia), the Privacy Commissioner (New Zealand) or the Information Regulator (South Africa).
