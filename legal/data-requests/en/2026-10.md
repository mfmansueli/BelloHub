# Your information: ask for a copy or deletion

**What this page is.** {{PROFESSIONAL_NAME}} keeps your name, contact details, appointments and, if you shared them, your health answers and photos. You can ask her for a copy, a correction or deletion at any time. This is also how you withdraw a consent.

**How to ask.** Choose what you want, write your name and phone number as you gave them when booking, and send. The request goes to her. She answers you directly, usually within a few days and within 30 days at the latest.

**What can you ask for.**
- **A copy** of what she holds about you, in a readable file.
- **A correction** of something that is wrong.
- **Deletion** of your information. She may have to keep some records for a period under professional or tax rules; she will tell you what and for how long.
- **To stop** a use you agreed to, such as photos in her portfolio.

**What Bello Hub holds.** Only your booking details (name, phone, optional email, service, time, status), which are deleted from our servers 7 days after your visit. Your health answers, signature and photos are not on our servers in readable form: they are with the professional. If you also want your booking details removed sooner, say so in the request.

**Identity.** To protect you, she may confirm that the request really comes from you, for example by sending a code to the phone number you booked with.

**If you are not satisfied.** You can complain to the data protection authority where you live. In the United States you may also contact your state attorney general. We do not treat you worse for making a request.
