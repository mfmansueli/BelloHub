# Legal documents

Seven documents were drafted, each for every localization (fourteen), under `legal/<document>/<localization>/<version>.md`.
**Four are in use.** The three consent documents (`consent-health`, `consent-photo`, `consent-guardian`) are **no longer produced**
(owner decision D55, 2026-10-09): consents are the professional's own business; the app only records their status. Their 2026-10 drafts stay here as history
and are shipped nowhere (not in the web bundle, not in the app bundle).
Written by Claude Code under the owner's decision D40 (open every market, formalize after sales): **drafts, not legal
advice, not reviewed by a lawyer**. The owner sends them to a lawyer if the product generates interest. Status of each
file is in [STATUS.md](STATUS.md), regenerated by `scripts/check-legal-status.sh --write`.

| Folder | Document | Who reads it |
|---|---|---|
| `terms-dpa` | Terms of use and data processing agreement | The professional, accepted at sign-up |
| `privacy-policy` | Privacy policy (app and website) | Everyone |
| `client-notice` | Notice on the booking page | A client booking online |
| `consent-health` | ~~Health-form consent~~ | Not produced (D55); draft kept as history |
| `consent-photo` | ~~Photo consent~~ | Not produced (D55); draft kept as history |
| `consent-guardian` | ~~Guardian consent for a minor~~ | Not produced (D55); draft kept as history |
| `data-requests` | Wording of copy and deletion requests | A client, in "Your information" |

`textVersion` in the app is the version in the file name (`2026-10`). Changing a text means a new file with a new
version; old versions stay, because the professional's acceptance of the terms stores the version it accepted.

## Fill-in fields (the owner supplies them once, a `Config/Legal/operator.json`, planned for the stage that builds the in-app legal screens)

`{{OPERATOR_NAME}}` the legal name of the service provider (today the individual developer account),
`{{OPERATOR_EMAIL}}` the privacy contact, `{{OPERATOR_ADDRESS}}` a postal address,
`{{WEB_BASE_URL}}` the site (`https://bellohub.app`), `{{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}` the date of the version.
Client-facing documents also use `{{PROFESSIONAL_NAME}}`, the professional's business name, filled by the app.

## What every text may say about the product (the facts sheet)

These are true of the build (`docs/06-PRIVACY-SECURITY.md`); a text must not say more, and must not say less than the law
needs in the market.

1. **Roles.** The professional is the controller of her clients' data. Bello Hub processes, as her processor, the booking
   data it hosts, and is itself the controller of the professional's own account data.
2. **Where clinical data lives.** Health answers, consent records, notes, photos and recordings live on the
   professional's iPhone and in her own iCloud, encrypted by the app with a key that is not in iCloud. Bello Hub's servers
   never hold them in readable form.
3. **What the servers hold.** Booking facts: the client's name, phone, optional email, the service, the time, the status,
   her language. Also a public profile the professional chose to publish, her phone number and plan, and push tokens.
4. **The health form.** It is encrypted in the client's browser with the professional's public key and stored as an opaque
   envelope, deleted when the professional's iPhone has received it and in any case after **30 days**. Bello Hub cannot read it.
5. **Retention.** Past bookings leave the server **7 days** after the visit. Logs contain no personal data. Deleting a
   client, or the account, also deletes the server data (account deletion completes within **30 days**; backups age out
   within a short window).
6. **Sub-processors.** Google (Firebase: database, functions, authentication, hosting, push messaging, storage), in the
   European Union; a transactional email provider; an SMS delivery network used by Firebase to send verification codes;
   Apple (iCloud for the professional's own vault, App Store billing).
7. **No analytics, no advertising, no cookies, no crash reporter that reads text.** The booking page stores only the
   chosen language in the browser.
8. **AI.** Dictation and organizing run on the iPhone. Nothing a professional says leaves the phone. The assistant never
   diagnoses and nothing is saved before she reviews it.
9. **Limits we state honestly.** The page that encrypts the form is served by Bello Hub, so a compromised server could in
   theory serve bad code; we reduce this with the controls described, but we do not claim it is impossible. We never claim
   absolute security, or legal compliance (`docs/06-PRIVACY-SECURITY.md` section 12).
10. **Money.** The only charge is the professional's own subscription, billed by Apple. Bello Hub processes no payments
    between professionals and clients. The first 10 client registrations are free (D42).
11. **Registration counter.** To keep the free allowance honest, an account keeps a counter and, in the professional's iCloud,
    a list of client ids and dates (no names or contact details), which stay when a client is deleted.
