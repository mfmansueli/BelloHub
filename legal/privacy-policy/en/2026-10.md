# Privacy policy

Effective {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}. This policy covers the Bello Hub app and the booking pages at {{WEB_BASE_URL}}.

## 1. Who we are
Bello Hub is operated by {{OPERATOR_NAME}}, {{OPERATOR_ADDRESS}}, contact {{OPERATOR_EMAIL}}. We play two roles. For the professional's own account we decide how the data is used. For the bookings and client data a professional enters, **the professional decides and we process it on her behalf**.

## 2. What we collect
- **Professional:** phone number (to sign in), name and business name, public profile she chooses to publish, plan and subscription status, push tokens, the language and region.
- **Booking client:** name, phone, optional email, the service and time, status, language, and what the professional records about the booking.
- **Health form:** only if the professional asks. It is encrypted in the client's browser and we cannot read it (section 4).
- **Nothing else.** No advertising identifiers, no analytics, no tracking cookies. The booking page remembers only the chosen language in the browser.

## 3. Why we use it
To run the app and the bookings; to send confirmations, reminders and codes; to bill the subscription through Apple; to keep the service safe and fix problems; and to meet legal duties. We do not sell personal information and do not share it for advertising.

## 4. Where clinical data lives
Health answers, signatures, notes, photos and recordings live on the professional's iPhone and in her own iCloud, encrypted by the app with a key that is not stored in iCloud. Our servers never hold them in readable form. A client's health form travels as an encrypted envelope that we keep only until the professional's iPhone has received it and never more than 30 days. If the professional loses her recovery phrase and her phone, we cannot recover this data for her, by design.

## 5. Who processes data for us
Google (Firebase: database, functions, authentication, hosting, messaging, storage) in the European Union; a transactional email provider; an SMS network that Firebase uses to send verification codes; and Apple (iCloud for the professional's vault, App Store billing). They process data only to provide their service.

## 6. International transfers
Our servers are in the European Union. If you use Bello Hub from elsewhere, your booking data is transferred to the European Union to run the service. Where the law requires, we rely on the safeguards each provider offers, such as standard contractual clauses.

## 7. How long we keep it
Past bookings leave our servers 7 days after the visit. Health-form envelopes are deleted on receipt and in any case within 30 days. When a client is deleted, or when you delete your account (completed within 30 days), the related server data is deleted; backups age out shortly afterwards. The professional keeps clinical data for as long as she chooses, subject to her own legal duties.

## 8. Security
Sensitive fields are encrypted on the device, the connection is encrypted, the database rules deny direct access, and the booking page that encrypts the health form is served from our domain. No system is perfectly secure; if a server were compromised, the page that does the encryption could in theory be altered, and we reduce this with the controls described in our security notes, but we cannot promise it is impossible.

## 9. AI
Dictation and organizing run on the iPhone. What a professional says does not leave the phone. The assistant never diagnoses and nothing is saved before she reviews it.

## 10. Your rights
You can ask to access, correct or delete your information, to restrict or object to its use, to receive a copy, and to withdraw a consent. Clients can use the page "Your information" or contact the professional; professionals can use the app. Write to {{OPERATOR_EMAIL}} for anything else. You can complain to your data protection authority. We do not treat you worse for using your rights.

## 11. Children
The service is for professionals and for clients who are 18 or older. A parent or guardian may book and consent for a minor. We do not knowingly collect information directly from anyone under 13.

## 12. United States
We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context advertising and do not use sensitive information beyond providing the service. Depending on your state (California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Washington and others) you may ask to know, access, delete, correct and opt out, and to appeal a decision. Consumer health information is collected only with consent, as the Washington My Health My Data Act and similar laws require.

## 13. Changes
When we change this policy in a way that matters, we tell you in the app and on this page before it applies, and keep old versions.
