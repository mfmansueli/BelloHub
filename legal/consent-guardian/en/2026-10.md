# Consent of a parent or legal guardian

**Who is asking.** {{PROFESSIONAL_NAME}} ("the professional") is asked to treat a person under 18, or under the age of adulthood where you live ("the minor"). Bello Hub is the app she uses to keep her records.

**Who should sign.** Only a parent or legal guardian of the minor. If you are not, please do not sign.

**What you are agreeing to.**
1. The professional may collect the minor's health information through the health form and use it only to decide whether and how to treat the minor safely and to keep a record of the treatments.
2. Photos, only if you tick the option below, under the same private rules as for adults: encrypted, in the professional's own records, never on Bello Hub's servers.
3. The professional may decide not to treat a minor, or to ask for the guardian to be present. Some treatments are not suitable for minors.

**Who can read it.** Only the professional. The health answers are encrypted in your browser before they are sent, so Bello Hub cannot read them; our servers keep the encrypted copy only until her iPhone has received it and never more than 30 days.

**Your choices.** You can ask for a copy, a correction or deletion of the minor's information at any time, and withdraw this consent by contacting the professional. When the minor reaches adulthood, the minor may take over these choices.

**Legal note (United States).** This is the verifiable consent of a parent or guardian for a minor's information. Bello Hub does not offer its service to children and does not collect information directly from anyone under 13.
