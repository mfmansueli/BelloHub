# How your booking information is used

**Who is responsible.** {{PROFESSIONAL_NAME}} is responsible for your information. Bello Hub, the app she uses, hosts your booking for her.

**What we ask.** Your name, your phone number, optionally your email, the service and the time you choose, and your language. We ask nothing else to book.

**Why.** To make, confirm and remind you of your appointment, and to contact you about it. The legal basis is that we need it to carry out the booking you request. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it and we do not share it with anyone else.

**Where it goes.** To {{PROFESSIONAL_NAME}} and to Bello Hub's servers in the European Union, only until 7 days after your visit. The service providers we use to send messages and host the page process it on our behalf. If you are in a country outside the European Union, your information is transferred to it only to the extent needed to make your booking.

**Health form (if she asks for one).** It is a separate step with questions only; any consent for your treatment is agreed with {{PROFESSIONAL_NAME}} directly, not on this page. Your answers are encrypted in your browser and Bello Hub cannot read them.

**Your rights.** You can ask for access, correction, erasure, restriction or objection at any time on the page "Your information" or by contacting {{PROFESSIONAL_NAME}}. You can complain to your data protection authority. More in the privacy policy at {{WEB_BASE_URL}}/privacy.
