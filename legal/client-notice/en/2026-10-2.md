# How your booking information is used

**Who is responsible.** {{PROFESSIONAL_NAME}} is responsible for your information. Bello Hub, the app she uses, hosts your booking for her.

**What we ask.** Your name, your phone number, your email (to send you a verification code and your confirmation), the service and the time you choose, and your language. We ask nothing else to book.

**Why.** To make, confirm and remind you of your appointment, and to contact you about it. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it and we do not share it with anyone else.

**Where it goes.** To {{PROFESSIONAL_NAME}} and to Bello Hub's servers in the European Union, only until 7 days after your visit. The service providers we use to send messages and host the page process it on our behalf.

**Health form (if she asks for one).** It is a separate step with questions only; any consent for your treatment is agreed with {{PROFESSIONAL_NAME}} directly, not on this page. Your answers are encrypted in your browser and Bello Hub cannot read them.

**Your rights.** You can ask for a copy, a correction or deletion at any time on the page "Your information" or by contacting {{PROFESSIONAL_NAME}}. More in the privacy policy at {{WEB_BASE_URL}}/privacy.

**US residents.** We do not sell or share your personal information for advertising and we do not use it to profile you. You can ask to access, delete or correct it, and we will not treat you differently for asking.
