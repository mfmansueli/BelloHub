## 1. The service

It is a record-keeping and scheduling tool. **It is not medical advice, does not diagnose, and does not replace your professional judgement, training or licence.** Bello Hub does not process payments between you and your clients.

## 2. Your account
You must be at least 18, use an iPhone running a supported iOS, and give accurate information. You are responsible for your phone number, your device and your **recovery phrase**. The recovery phrase is the only way to restore your encrypted records if you lose your phone. We cannot recover it or your records for you.

## 3. Price and free allowance
Your first 10 client registrations are free. Deleted clients still count toward this limit. Registrations are counted across manual entry, import and online booking; duplicates and edits do not count. After the tenth, adding another client needs a subscription. Existing clients, appointments and records always stay available and editable; we never delete or lock your data because you do not subscribe. The paid plan has unlimited client registrations and the same features monthly or yearly. Prices are shown in the App Store before you buy and include VAT where it applies. Billing, renewal and cancellation are handled by Apple and follow Apple's rules; manage them in your Apple account settings.

You must have a lawful basis to collect it, give your clients the notice and obtain the consents the law requires (Bello Hub provides texts you can use, drafted without a lawyer's review, which you should check against the law that applies to you), keep it accurate, keep it only as long as needed and respond to your clients' requests. Where you are required to, you must register with the data protection regulator (for example the ICO fee in the United Kingdom). You must hold any licence your work requires and follow the rules of your profession.
