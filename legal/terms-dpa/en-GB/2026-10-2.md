# Part A. Common part

## A1. The service
Bello Hub is an iPhone app and a set of booking pages that help independent beauty and wellness professionals run their appointments and keep their clients' records. It is a record-keeping and scheduling tool. **It is not medical advice, does not diagnose, and does not replace your professional judgement, training or licence.** Bello Hub processes no payments between you and your clients; prices are information only.

## A2. Your account
You must be at least 18, use an iPhone with a supported iOS version and give accurate information. You sign in with Apple or with your phone number. You are responsible for your Apple Account or phone number, your device and your **recovery key**, the twelve words that are the only way to restore your encrypted records on a new phone. We never see the recovery key and cannot recover it or your records for you.

## A3. Price and free allowance
Your first 10 client registrations are free. Deleted clients still count towards this limit. Registrations are counted across manual entry, import and online booking; duplicates and edits do not count. After the tenth, adding another client needs a subscription. Your existing clients, appointments and records always stay available and editable; we never delete or lock your data because you do not subscribe. The subscription has unlimited client registrations and the same features whether paid monthly or yearly, with no trial. Prices are shown in the App Store before you buy and include VAT where it applies. Billing, renewal and cancellation are handled by Apple under Apple's terms; you manage them in your Apple Account.

## A4. Your responsibilities
You are the controller of your clients' information. You must have a lawful basis for collecting it, give your clients the information the law requires, obtain any consent you need (Bello Hub provides no consent text and collects no signature: the app only lets you record that a consent exists and attach a photo or file of it), keep the information accurate and only for as long as you need it, and answer your clients' requests. You must hold any licence your work requires and follow the rules of your profession. You will not use Bello Hub for anything unlawful, to harass anyone, to send unsolicited messages, or to store information about people who have not been told you hold it.

## A5. Health information
Collect only the health information you need to treat your client safely. It is special category data. It stays on your iPhone and in your iCloud, encrypted; when a client fills in your health form online, it is sealed in her browser and only your iPhone can open it.

## A6. Data processing agreement
This section applies to the personal data of your clients that Bello Hub processes for you, and is the contract required by Article 28 of the GDPR and of the UK GDPR.
1. **Subject and duration.** Hosting your public booking page, your bookings and requests, and the sealed mailbox for what clients send you, for as long as your account exists and then for the deletion periods of the privacy policy.
2. **Data and people.** Your clients' name, phone number, email address, the service, time, status, notes they write on the page and their language; sealed envelopes we cannot open.
3. **Instructions.** We process the data only on your documented instructions, which are these terms and your use of the app, and for no purpose of our own, unless the law requires it; in that case we tell you first where the law allows.
4. **Confidentiality.** Anyone working for us who can access the data is bound by confidentiality. We cannot read the sealed envelopes.
5. **Security.** We apply the measures described in the privacy policy, appropriate to the risk (Article 32).
6. **Sub-processors.** You give general authorisation for the sub-processors listed in the privacy policy. We tell you in the app before we add or replace one, and you may object by closing your account. We bind each of them to the same obligations.
7. **Your clients' requests.** We help you answer requests of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection; the app lets you do most of them yourself, and clients can send you their requests from their booking page.
8. **Incidents.** We tell you without undue delay, and within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, of a personal data breach affecting your clients' data, with what we know and what we are doing about it.
9. **Deletion and return.** When you delete a client or close your account, we delete the related data within the periods of the privacy policy; you can export everything first, on your iPhone.
10. **Audits.** We give you the information needed to show that we meet these obligations and allow reasonable audits, at most once a year, with notice, during working hours and without exposing other customers' data.
11. **Transfers.** Our servers are in the European Union. Any other transfer relies on an adequacy decision or on standard contractual clauses.
